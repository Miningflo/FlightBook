FlightBook privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

FlightBook is a personal pilot logbook that runs entirely in your browser. It has no server of its own: the developer does not receive, store or see your logbook or any other data about you.

Data stored on your device

Flights, aircraft, settings and cached photos are stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB). They stay on your device unless you export them or connect Google Drive. Clearing the site's data in your browser deletes them.

Google user data

If you choose to connect Google Drive, FlightBook asks for one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This only gives access to files that FlightBook itself created in your Drive (the "Logbook backups" folder and its contents), not to any other file in your Drive. FlightBook does not request your e-mail address, profile, contacts or any other Google data.

  • What is accessed: the backup snapshots and the master.json file that FlightBook writes.
  • How it is used: only to back up your logbook and to restore or view it on your own devices.
  • Where it goes: directly between your browser and Google. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any other party.
  • Sharing: Google user data is not sold, shared, transferred or used for advertising, and it is not used to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • Retention and deletion: the files stay in your Google Drive under your control. You can delete them in Drive at any time. "Disconnect Google Drive" in FlightBook's settings revokes access; you can also remove FlightBook at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

FlightBook's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services the app contacts

  • Aircraft photos: when showing a photo for an aircraft, the app sends the aircraft registration (e.g. OO-ABC) to Planespotters.net and Wikimedia Commons, and loads the image from them.
  • Airport data: when you import airports, the app downloads the public OurAirports data file from GitHub.

These services receive the usual technical information of any web request (such as your IP address) under their own privacy policies. No logbook entries are sent to them.

Hosting

The app is served by GitHub Pages, which may log technical request data under GitHub's privacy statement. FlightBook uses no analytics, cookies for tracking, or advertising.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/Miningflo/FlightBook.
